# **Tennis Performance Report**

## **Performance DNA**
- Technical Score: 76/100
- Tactical Score: 73/100

**Summary:** The player demonstrates solid fundamental stability with a high Balance Score of 82% and effective energy transfer (Kinetic Chain Efficiency 78%). However, the technique lacks explosiveness, evidenced by a critically low Racket Head Speed of 27.6 MPH, while Footwork Efficiency at 75% suggests suboptimal positioning that is impacting Stroke Consistency (71%).

## **Key Strengths**
- High Balance Score of 82% maintains stability through contact, notably seen in the backhand at Frame 95
- Strong Kinetic Chain Efficiency at 78% allows for fluid movement and energy transfer
- Forehand is the dominant wing with an average quality of 76% across 2 detected shots

## **Areas to Improve**

- Backhand Stroke Quality lags at 68% across 3 shots, requiring technical stabilization

## **Training Plan**

**Speed-Gate Feeding**
Rapid-fire feeding drill focusing on increasing Racket Head Speed above the current 27.6 MPH baseline by enforcing shorter preparation times and faster core unloading.

**X-Pattern Recovery Drill**
High-intensity footwork drill targeting the 75% efficiency score. Player must hit corners and recover to specific center targets to enforce precise split-steps and eliminate lazy recovery steps.

**Core Torque Resistance**
Resistance band rotation exercises to boost Core Rotation Speed from 145°/s to 160°/s+, directly addressing the power leak on the lower-quality (68%) backhand side.
