Medicine ball throws mimicking groundstrokes to increase Core Rotation Speed above the current 145°/s and drive Racket Head Speed up.

**Cone Recovery Sprints**
High-intensity intervals focusing on the first crossover step to improve Footwork Efficiency from 75% to 85%.

**Deep Backhand Isolation**
Feed rapid deep balls to the backhand corner to improve stability and raise the 68% backhand quality and 71% stroke consistency.
