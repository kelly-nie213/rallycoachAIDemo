# **Tennis Performance Report**

## **Performance DNA**
- Technical Score: 74/100
- Tactical Score: 72/100

**Summary:** The player exhibits strong postural stability with a Balance Score of 82%, particularly evident during the change of direction in frames 65-70. However, the Racket Head Speed of 27.6 MPH is critically low for elite play, likely caused by a Footwork Efficiency of 75% that forces reactive rather than proactive striking positions.

## **Key Strengths**
- High stability with a Balance Score of 82%, maintaining verticality even during wide defensive shots (Frames 140-150)
- Solid Kinetic Chain Efficiency at 78%, showing connected movement despite the lack of acceleration
- Forehand Avg Quality of 76% proves to be the more reliable wing compared to the backhand

## **Areas to Improve**
- Critically low Racket Head Speed at 27.6 MPH limits offensive capability and ability to dictate points
- Footwork Efficiency at 75% indicates excessive adjustment steps, particularly observed in the recovery phase between frames 100-115
- Backhand Avg Quality of 68% is the primary liability, struggling with depth and pace generation

## **Training Plan**

**Rotational Velocity Overload**
Medicine ball rotational throws focusing on hip-shoulder separation to increase Core Rotation Speed from the current 145°/s.

**Split-Step Timing & Recovery**
High-intensity shadow stroking focusing on the first step to improve Footwork Efficiency (75%) and reduce distance traveled per shot.

**Overspeed Band Work**
Resistance band shadow swings emphasizing wrist lag and snap to elevate Racket Head Speed significantly above 27.6 MPH.
